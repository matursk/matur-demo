Matur – Android Beta Website (React/Vite) – In‑Depth Content Summary
===============================================================

1. Overall purpose and audience
-------------------------------

The website presents “Matur”, a Slovak Android application designed to help secondary‑school students
prepare for their school‑leaving exams (“maturity”). The site’s public part serves mainly as:

- A landing page explaining what the app does and why it exists.
- An announcement and information hub for a closed Android beta programme.
- An FAQ for prospective or accepted beta testers.
- A legal and privacy section (Beta policy and closed‑beta terms, CBETA).

A separate, login‑protected section (“CBETA”) is aimed at invited testers. It provides a simple
dashboard with APK download instructions, links to the bug‑reporting console and the contractual
terms governing the closed beta.

The site is written in Slovak and is built with React + TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind‑style utility
classes, Firebase (authentication, hosting/storage, analytics) and Cloudflare (via wrangler config).


2. Public homepage (“Root” route)
---------------------------------

### 2.1 Hero section and main value proposition

The homepage opens with the slogan:

- “Zmaturuj s ľahkosťou” – roughly “Pass your maturita with ease”.

Visitors are invited to join the Android beta programme, with the explicit promise that the app will
help them prepare for the exams “simply and quickly”. Immediately, the site states that **beta
sign‑ups are already closed** (“Prihlasovanie uzavreté”).

The hero and first content blocks stress three main selling points:

1. **Direct focus on maturita exams**
   - Content and exercises are tied directly to maturita topics and exam areas.
   - Includes model exam questions, step‑by‑step solution procedures, explanations of correct
     answers and strategy tips.
   - Short quizzes with instant feedback help students quickly identify what they already know and
     what needs more practice.

2. **Adapted to students’ real life**
   - Lessons are deliberately short and easy to understand, with language stripped of unnecessary
     “balast”.
   - The app uses *adaptive repetition* based on the user’s mistakes.
   - It can send reminders before important dates.
   - There are study modes tailored to 10, 20 or 30‑minute sessions so that students can fit
     practice around other obligations.

3. **Help to actually succeed, not just “provide content”**
   - The app tracks progress and offers an overview of how the student is doing.
   - There are daily goals, streaks (series of days in a row) and badges to support motivation.
   - Topic recommendations are driven by the student’s results.
   - The app sends intelligent reminders.
   - During the beta period, testers are encouraged to report bugs directly in the app; this is
     framed as a collaborative process where the app “improves together with you”.

Overall, the homepage paints Matur as a focused, exam‑oriented learning companion that combines
structured content with motivational and adaptive features.

### 2.2 Beta programme: capacity, duration and participation

The homepage then offers a concise description of the **beta plan and capacity**:

- Capacity: **10 seats** in the closed beta.
- Status: **beta is running** (“Beta je spustená”) and will continue **until 1.11.2025**.
- Selected users receive an APK build of the app via e‑mail.
- After installation they log in using the e‑mail address provided in the application form.
- Their task is to actually use the app for maturita preparation and to report bugs through the
  integrated bug‑reporting flow (in‑app Settings → “Nahlásiť chybu”).

A reward scheme is explicitly mentioned:

- For every **confirmed bug report**, the team plans to pay **5 EUR**. This incentivises thorough and
  precise reporting.

### 2.3 Roadmap and product evolution

A dedicated “Roadmapa” section visually and textually outlines the app’s development timeline:

- **1.9.2025 – Idea**  
  The idea to create an app to help maturita students is born.

- **5.9.2025 – First steps**  
  The first app skeleton is created – essentially a bare bones structure.

- **11.9.2025 – Prototype**  
  A first functional prototype appears, with settings and basic features.

- **20.9.2025 – Alpha**  
  Initial alpha feedback is collected from friends. An admin console for lessons and questions is
  created.

- **28.9.2025 – Preparation for closed beta**  
  Key features are completed and closed beta preparations are finalised.

- **“Teraz” – First (closed) beta**  
  The closed beta is currently running, actively testing with users and collecting feedback.

- **December 2025 – Q1 2026 – Public beta**  
  Planned public beta release with full access, while work on the final version continues.

- **Q2 – Q3 2026 – Full release**  
  Planned official launch of the app.

- **Future – Additional subjects**  
  Long‑term goal: apps and content for more maturita subjects, covering all aspects of exam
  preparation.

This roadmap sets expectations for testers, shows ongoing progress and provides a sense of strategic
direction beyond the initial version.

### 2.4 FAQ: practical details for testers

The homepage also includes an FAQ in Slovak that answers typical questions:

- **How do I get access?**  
  Selected participants are contacted by e‑mail with an APK download link.

- **Is the beta only for Android?**  
  Yes. The app is currently distributed as an APK only. It is not yet on Google Play or the App
  Store, and iOS is not supported.

- **Is the beta free?**  
  Yes. Participation is free of charge; testers “pay” by providing feedback.

- **How do I install the APK?**  
  Users must enable installation from unknown sources in Android settings, then open the link from
  the e‑mail and install the APK. A detailed guide will be sent together with the download link.

- **What are the minimum requirements?**  
  Android 8.0 or higher and at least 100 MB of free storage. Older devices may have lower
  performance.

- **How do I report bugs and claim a reward?**  
  Bugs are reported directly in the app (Settings → “Nahlásiť chybu”), including a clear description
  and reproduction steps. Confirmed bugs are planned to be rewarded with **5 EUR**.

- **How long does the selection process take?**  
  Selection is gradual and depends on capacity and testing needs. Results are communicated via
  e‑mail.

- **What happens to my data?**  
  Data is used only for organising the beta and communication. The FAQ explicitly refers to the
  detailed **Beta policy** and notes that data will be deleted after the beta ends, in line with the
  policy.

- **Can I invite friends?**  
  Testers can share the sign‑up link, but the number of participants is limited.

- **Who can I contact with questions?**  
  There is a support e‑mail: **podpora@matur.sk**.


3. Beta sign‑up page (“Beta” route)
-----------------------------------

The dedicated beta route currently states that **sign‑ups for the closed beta are closed**. It
addresses users who already applied:

- It confirms that registration is closed and distribution is being prepared.
- It tells applicants that if they signed up, they will receive an e‑mail with further steps.
- It promises an e‑mail containing the APK download link and installation instructions once the
  build is ready.
- It points users to the **Beta policy** page for more details on data processing.

Originally, this page also hosted the application form; now it serves as a status and information
page for applicants awaiting response.


4. Legal and privacy pages
--------------------------

### 4.1 “Beta policy”

This page is a concise privacy and participation policy for the beta:

- **Purpose of beta testing**  
  To verify the app’s functionality, stability, performance and usability before wider release.

- **Eligible participants**  
  Primarily **secondary‑school students preparing for maturita** (approx. 17–19 years). Access is
  capacity‑limited and granted manually.

- **Scope of processed data**  
  - Identification: first and last name.
  - Contact: e‑mail.
  - Study data: school, grade/year, city/region.
  - Technical: device model and operating system.
  - Optional: maturita subjects, reasons for interest.
  - Operational: date and time of application.

- **Purpose and retention**  
  Data is used solely for organising the beta, communicating with participants, conducting testing
  analytics and improving the product. Data will be stored **for the duration of the beta**, at the
  latest until **1.11.2025**, unless law or legitimate interest requires a longer period.

- **Legal basis**  
  The legal basis is the participant’s consent, expressed by submitting the beta application. Consent
  can be withdrawn at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

- **Use of processors / third parties**  
  The project may use service providers (e.g. hosting, e‑mail). These processors act only on the
  controller’s instructions and under appropriate security measures.

- **User rights**  
  Participants are informed of their rights:
  - access to data,
  - rectification,
  - erasure (“right to be forgotten”),
  - objection and restriction of processing,
  - withdrawal of consent.

  Rights can be exercised by contacting **podpora@matur.sk**.

- **Beta‑specific disclaimers**  
  The policy emphasises that beta versions may contain errors, may be unstable and that features or
  content can change without notice. The beta is not intended for production use.

- **Data controller**  
  The controller is “Matur”, contact **podpora@matur.sk**.

- **Validity and updates**  
  The policy is effective from the date of publication and may be updated. The current version is
  always available on this page. Users already accepted into the closed beta are asked to also read
  the **CBETA terms**.

### 4.2 Closed‑beta terms of service (CBETA)

The CBETA page defines contractual conditions between the app operator (“Prevádzkovateľ”) and the
Tester in the closed beta. Key points:

- **Definitions**  
  CBETA is a closed, invite‑only testing phase. Test builds are experimental and may contain bugs,
  unfinished features and diagnostic tools. Feedback includes bug reports, suggestions, logs,
  screenshots and other information. “Neverejné informácie” (non‑public information) covers all
  non‑public product details, roadmaps, pricing, internal processes and data obtained in testing.

- **Eligibility and access**  
  Access is granted individually by the operator, who may refuse or revoke access at any time without
  reason. Testers must be legally competent; minors need parental consent. Access is non‑transferable
  and testers must not share installers or credentials.

- **Confidentiality (NDA)**  
  Testers must keep all non‑public information confidential during participation and for 3 years
  after CBETA ends (or until the information becomes public without breach). They must not publish or
  share screenshots, videos, feature descriptions, performance measurements, etc. without prior
  written consent. Breach of confidentiality may lead to termination and possible claims.

- **Feedback and intellectual property**  
  Testers grant the operator a broad licence to use feedback (including ideas and suggestions) for
  improving the product, without any obligation to provide compensation.  

- **Use of test builds**  
  Builds are provided solely for evaluation, internal testing and product improvement. They are not
  for production use. Testers must not reverse‑engineer, decompile, bypass security mechanisms or
  redistribute test builds. Updates should be installed without undue delay; older builds may be
  deactivated.

- **Compatibility and technical requirements**  
  CBETA may be limited to selected Android versions and devices; compatibility is not guaranteed and
  may change. Testers are responsible for backing up their own data and securing their device.

- **Data protection in CBETA**  
  Personal data processing follows the Beta policy and applicable law. CBETA may additionally collect
  extended diagnostics (crash reports, performance metrics, aggregated interaction data) solely to
  improve quality and stability. The legal basis is tester consent and/or the operator’s legitimate
  interest.

- **Warranties and limitation of liability**  
  Test builds are provided “as is”, without any explicit or implied warranties, including fitness for
  a particular purpose or uninterrupted availability. The operator’s overall liability to a tester is
  capped at **50 EUR**, except where mandatory law requires otherwise. The operator is not liable for
  lost profit, data loss, business interruption or indirect / consequential damage.

- **Indemnification**  
  Testers agree to indemnify the operator against third‑party claims resulting from their breach of
  CBETA terms or infringement of third‑party rights.

- **Duration and termination**  
  CBETA terms take effect when a tester is admitted and remain valid until their participation ends.
  The operator can terminate participation with immediate effect; testers can end participation by
  uninstalling test builds and notifying the operator. After termination, testers must delete test
  builds and stop using non‑public information.

- **Governing law, disputes and changes**  
  Slovak law applies; disputes fall under Slovak courts at the operator’s seat. The operator may
  modify CBETA terms at any time; changes take effect once published and continued participation
  implies acceptance. If any provision is invalid, the rest remain in force. The document forms the
  entire agreement regarding CBETA. The contact for questions is again **podpora@matur.sk**.


5. CBETA dashboard and authentication
-------------------------------------

The `/cbeta` section is a small authenticated “portal” for invited testers:

- **Login and signup**  
  - Login page: e‑mail + password form, with error display.
  - Signup page: checks whether the e‑mail is on an allowlist. If not allowed, access is denied with
    a message that the e‑mail is not authorised for the closed CBETA and the user can try another
    account.
  - When allowed, the page explains password requirements (at least 12 characters, combination of
    uppercase, lowercase, number and symbol).

- **Dashboard**  
  The dashboard shows:
  - A CBETA overview header and a logout option.
  - An **APK download** block:
    - On mobile, testers can register and download via Firebase App Distribution.
    - On desktop, they are told to open the page on Android or scan a QR code with their phone.
  - A link to the **CBETA terms** page (“Podmienky CBETA”), with a note that they should be read
    before use.
  - A **bug console** block linking to a separate bug‑tracking site (`bugs.matur.sk`) where testers
    can claim rewards for bugs reported from within the app and see the status of their reports.

Together, this closed area implements the operational workflow of the beta: controlled enrolment,
distribution of test builds and structured bug reporting, all bound by the legal and privacy
framework described above.
